Donor (Giver)

The person transferring the asset. Must be at least 21 years old and of full mental capacity.

The donor must be acting freely and voluntarily. If there is any doubt about undue influence, the deed should be executed before an independent solicitor.

Donee (Recipient)

The person receiving the gift.

A minor cannot hold property directly. A parent or guardian must accept and hold the gift on trust until the donee turns 21.
Guardian details
ABSD alert: A Singapore Citizen who already owns one residential property pays 20% ABSD on a second. A PR pays 30%. Please verify with a tax adviser.

Property details
HDB: HDB's prior written approval is required before this deed takes effect. Both parties must satisfy HDB's eligibility criteria. A mortgaged HDB flat cannot be gifted — it must be sold instead.
CPF refund: The Donor must refund the CPF principal plus accrued interest to their CPF account upon transfer. This is mandatory.
CPF refund required on transfer. Verify the CPF withdrawal amount + accrued interest with CPF Board.
A mortgaged property generally cannot be gifted without the mortgagee bank's prior written consent. You may need to redeem the mortgage first or restructure the transaction as a partial sale. Please seek legal advice.

Stamp Duty
Stamp duty reminder: Any gift of property or shares must be stamped by IRAS within 14 days if executed in Singapore (30 days if overseas). Duty is assessed on open market value, not on zero consideration. Failure to stamp attracts penalties up to 4× the duty.

Witnesses & Review

Best practice is to have separate witnesses for the Donor and Donee — each witness present when their respective signatory signs.

Each witness must be an independent adult who is not a party to the deed, not a beneficiary, and not a close relative of any party.
